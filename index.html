News Harvest Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 1, 2026

News Harvest provides agricultural news and information to farmers, ranchers, and others interested in agriculture. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the News Harvest app.

Information We Collect

News Harvest may process limited technical information necessary to operate the app, provide content, maintain app functionality, and communicate with its servers.

Certain app preferences, including saved articles and settings, may be stored locally on your device.

Subscriptions and Payments

If you purchase a News Harvest subscription through Google Play, payment processing is handled by Google Play. News Harvest does not directly receive or store your full payment card information.
