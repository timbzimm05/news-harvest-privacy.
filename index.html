News Harvest Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 1, 2026

News Harvest provides agricultural news and information to farmers, ranchers, and others interested in agriculture. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the News Harvest app.

Information We Collect

News Harvest may process limited technical information necessary to operate the app, provide content, maintain app functionality, and communicate with its servers.

Certain app preferences, including saved articles and settings, may be stored locally on your device.

Subscriptions and Payments

If you purchase a News Harvest subscription through Google Play, payment processing is handled by Google Play. News Harvest does not directly receive or store your full payment card information.

News and Third-Party Content News Harvest aggregates and displays links, headlines, images, and other information from third-party news and agricultural information sources. When you open a third-party article, you may be directed to the publisher's website. Those websites have their own privacy policies and practices, which News Harvest does not control.

Location and Grain Price Information

News Harvest may use location information that you provide, such as a ZIP code, to retrieve relevant local agricultural information and grain prices. This information may also be stored locally on your device to remember your preferences.

Third-Party Services

News Harvest may communicate with third-party services to provide news, agricultural information, grain market information, app functionality, and subscription services. These third-party services may process technical information according to their own privacy policies.

Data Sharing

News Harvest does not sell your personal information. Information may be processed or shared with service providers only when necessary to operate and provide the app's features or when required by law.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information associated with News Harvest. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

News Harvest is not directed toward children under 13, and News Harvest does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Choices

You may remove locally stored app information by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. Subscriptions purchased through Google Play can be managed or canceled through your Google Play account.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact News Harvest using the developer contact information provided on the Google Play listing.
